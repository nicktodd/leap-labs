# Demo: Module 13 - Week 2 Wrap-up & Assessment Prep (Pipelines)

**Duration:** 8 minutes
**Prerequisite:** none, this is a recap. No new tooling or commands.

## Part 1: What Friday's assessment actually checks (2 min)

Narration: two things, a Git repo submission and a short presentation, covering your agile
workflow and your secure coding approach. Both are things you've already produced this week,
Friday isn't asking for anything new, it's checking that what you built during the sprint is
genuinely in a submittable state.

## Part 2: Model a peer-check (4 min)

Pick a volunteer pair. Run through a few checklist items live:

```text
"Show me your team repo's commit history, do the messages follow your Foundations week's
Module 6 convention?"
"Open a merged PR, show me a review comment on it."
"Show me your Mission Day findings, can you name the OWASP category for each one?"
"Walk me through your Terraform config from Module 7, can you explain what each resource does?"
```

Narration: same spirit as the Foundations week's equivalent module, a peer-check isn't a test, it's a second
pair of eyes while there's still time to fix a gap.

## Part 3: Naming this week's throughline (2 min)

Narration: the Foundations week built the tooling and ways-of-working foundations. This week (Pipelines) built
the practices that make a team actually trustworthy with those tools: DevOps and CI/CD discipline,
reviewed changes, and security awareness. Mission Day proved all of it works together under real time pressure.
That combination, tools plus practices plus the discipline to use them under pressure, is what
Friday is actually assessing, not any single technical fact.

## Key message

This module has no new content on purpose. It exists to catch gaps while there's still time to
close them, and to make Friday's assessment a formality rather than a surprise.
