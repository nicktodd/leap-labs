# Module 6 Lab — Peer Review

## Objectives

By the end of this lab you will have:

- Reviewed a partner's Module 4-5 design (class diagram + sequence diagram) using a structured
  checklist
- Left at least two specific, constructive written comments
- Given either an approval or a change request, with clear reasoning
- Practised receiving review comments on your own design without defensiveness

## Setup

- Your own `mission-model.mmd` (Module 4) and `mission-order-sequence.mmd` (Module 5)
- A partner, ideally someone you haven't already discussed your design with in detail
- [`review-checklist.md`](review-checklist.md) from this lab

## Task

1. **Swap designs**
   Give your partner access to your `mission-model.mmd` and `mission-order-sequence.mmd` (and
   the rendered `.png`s, if that's easier to read), and get access to theirs.

2. **Review your partner's design**
   Using `review-checklist.md`, work through both diagrams together.
   - Leave **at least two** specific, constructive comments — written down, not just said aloud
   - Decide: would you approve this design, or request changes? Write one sentence explaining why

3. **Receive your partner's review**
   Read the comments left on your own design.
   - For each one, decide: do you agree, and if so, would you action it?
   - If you disagree, write a short, non-defensive reply explaining your reasoning — "I chose
     this because X, does that address your concern?", not "no, you're wrong"

4. **Discuss**
   As a pair, talk through one comment each of you received. Was it clear what to do? Did it feel
   constructive?

## What to do with the feedback

You don't need to redo your Module 4-5 diagrams today. Keep the review comments — Modules 7-8
(SOLID) will give you the vocabulary and technique to actually fix the issues your reviewer
found, and Module 14's retrospective will ask what changed between this draft and what you
eventually built.

## Acceptance criteria

- At least two written comments exist on your partner's design, each specific enough to act on
- You've given an explicit approve/change-request decision with a reason
- You've written at least one considered response to a comment on your own design
- You can name one thing from the checklist you hadn't thought to check before this module

If you finish early, swap again with a different pair and repeat — does the checklist feel
faster the second time?
