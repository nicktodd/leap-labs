# Demo: Module 11 - Week 1 Wrap-up & Assessment Prep

**Duration:** 6 minutes
**Prerequisite:** none, this is a recap. No new tooling or commands.

## Part 1: What Friday actually checks (2 min)

Narration: walk through the assessment checklist categories on the slides (Environment &
GenAI, Agile & Ceremonies, Git & GitHub, PR & Code Review, Team Repo). Be explicit: Friday's
assessment is checking that the *foundations* from this week are solid, not testing anything
new. If you can tick every item on the checklist honestly, you're ready.

## Part 2: Model a peer-check (3 min)

Pick a volunteer. Run through two or three checklist items live as a demonstration of what a
peer-check conversation should sound like:

```text
"Show me git remote -v in your team repo, what does origin point at?"
"Show me a real PR you opened and a real review comment you left on someone else's."
"Open your team repo's README, read me the Branching Strategy section."
```

Narration: a peer-check isn't a test, it's a second pair of eyes. If your partner can't show
you something, that's useful information *now*, while there's still time to fix it, not on
Friday morning.

## Part 3: Naming this week's throughline (1 min)

Narration: close by naming the pattern that ran through the whole week. Every skill introduced
(Agile ceremonies, Git, GitHub, pull requests, peer review) followed the same shape: understand
the concept, practise the mechanics, and where relevant, use GenAI as a learning aid to speed up
understanding an unfamiliar piece of syntax or an error message, but always verify before
trusting it. That pattern doesn't stop after this week, it's how you'll approach every new tool
for the rest of the programme - starting next week, when CI/CD, Docker, and security join the
same Git and GitHub habits you've just built.

## Key message

This module has no new content on purpose. It exists to catch gaps while there's still time to
close them, and to make Friday's assessment a formality rather than a surprise.
