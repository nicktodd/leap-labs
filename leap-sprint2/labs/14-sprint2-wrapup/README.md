# Module 14 Lab — Sprint 2 Wrap-up & Assessment Prep

## Objectives

By the end of this lab you will have:

- Reviewed what Friday's Sprint 2 assessment will check
- Identified and closed any remaining gaps in OWASP knowledge, Git hygiene, or pipeline setup
- Peer-checked a partner's repo and assessment artefacts against the checklist below
- Confirmed your team repo is in a clean, submittable state

## Setup

- Everything from Modules 01-13, nothing new
- A partner, ideally not from your own team

## What Friday actually assesses

Two things: a **Git repo submission** and a **short presentation** covering your agile
workflow and your secure coding approach. Both are things your team has already produced, this
week; Friday checks they're genuinely submittable, not that you can produce something new.

## The assessment checklist

For each item, your partner should *show* you the thing, not just tell you it's done.

### Agile & Ceremonies (Modules 01-02)

- [ ] Can name the three Scrum roles and one responsibility of each
- [ ] Can show a real sprint goal their team wrote (Module 02's lab or Mission Day)
- [ ] Can explain Start / Stop / Continue and show real retro notes from this sprint

### Git & Review (Modules 03-04)

- [ ] Team repo has a merged PR with a real reviewer comment on it, not just an approval click
- [ ] Commit messages follow a stated convention (Conventional Commits or the team's own)
- [ ] Can explain what branch protection rule is configured (or would be) on `main`

### DevOps, CI/CD, and IaC (Modules 05-08)

- [ ] Can explain the difference between CI, Delivery, and Deployment in one sentence each
- [ ] Team's Jenkins Multibranch Pipeline job exists and has built at least one PR automatically
- [ ] Can point to the multi-stage Dockerfile and explain why it's smaller/safer than a single-stage build

### Security (Modules 09-11)

- [ ] Can name at least five OWASP Top 10 (2025) categories from memory, unprompted
- [ ] Has completed Secure Code Warrior onboarding and at least two challenges
- [ ] Has a real example of correcting a Copilot suggestion during Module 11 or Mission Day

### SRE (Module 12)

- [ ] Can correctly spell out SLI, SLO, and SLA in full
- [ ] Can calculate an error budget from a stated SLO, live, on request

### Mission Day (Module 13)

- [ ] Can name every vulnerability their team found, with its OWASP category
- [ ] Team repo shows the Mission Day fixes committed via proper branches and PRs
- [ ] Presentation from Mission Day is ready to show again if asked

## Task sheet

1. Pair up with a partner, ideally from outside your own team.
2. Work through every checklist category together, partner by partner.
3. Write down specific gaps, not vague ones ("couldn't show a real reviewer comment on a PR,"
   not "Git stuff").
4. Use remaining time to close your own gaps.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every item on the checklist has been shown, not just claimed, to a partner.
- You have a specific, written list of any gaps you personally still have.
- For each gap, you've either closed it or know exactly what you'll do before Friday.

Friday: guest speaker, Weekly Knowledge Check (MCQ), Sprint 2 Assessment, Group Retro.
