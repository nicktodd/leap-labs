# Module 01 Lab — Agile & Scrum Fundamentals

## Objectives

By the end of this lab you will have:

- Reviewed a sample product backlog and judged which items are ready for sprint planning
- Applied the INVEST criteria to justify your judgement
- Written acceptance criteria for two stories that are missing them

## Setup

- The [`sample-backlog.md`](sample-backlog.md) from this lab, a fictional mobile banking
  backlog

## Task sheet

1. **Read the whole backlog** in `sample-backlog.md` before judging anything, some items only
   make sense in relation to others.

2. **Assess readiness**
   For every item (PS-101 to PS-110), decide: is it ready for sprint planning, or not? Use
   INVEST (Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable) to justify each
   decision in one sentence. Be specific about *which* INVEST criterion an item fails, "it's
   vague" isn't specific enough.

3. **Write acceptance criteria**
   Pick two items from your "not ready" list that fail mainly because they're missing
   acceptance criteria (not because they're too big or too vague to fix quickly). Write 3-4
   acceptance criteria for each, in the same style as the ready items already in the backlog.

4. **Flag the outliers**
   At least one item in this backlog isn't really a "story" at all, it's a technical initiative
   dressed up as one. Find it and explain why treating it like a normal story would cause
   problems for sprint planning.

## Acceptance criteria (for this lab)

- You have a readiness judgement and an INVEST-based justification for all 10 backlog items.
- You've written acceptance criteria for two previously-incomplete stories.
- You've identified and explained the item that isn't really a well-formed story.

If you finish early, discuss with a partner: did you agree on which items were ready? Where you
disagreed, was it a genuine judgement call, or did one of you miss something in the item's
description?
